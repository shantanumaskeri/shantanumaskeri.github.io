Hi! My name is Shantanu.

I am a Unity Engineer.

With almost two decades of Software Engineering experience (13 yrs in Unity), I build Apps, Games, XR Experiences, Experiential Activations, Websites, for clients both local & international, ranging from Indie Studios to AAA organizations.
